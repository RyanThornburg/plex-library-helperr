Movers

The Movers & profilers job. Movers move matching media to another root folder, with its files; they run in order and the first match wins, so a later mover never sees what an earlier one matched. Profilers set the quality profile (and optionally monitoring) of matching media. It runs from Runs, a schedule, or Radarr's and Sonarr's webhook when media is added.
