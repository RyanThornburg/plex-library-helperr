Removers

The remover rules in config.toml. With auto-approve on, a removers run that uses config.toml's settings (Run…, or a schedule without "Force dry run") deletes that rule's removals without review. Forced dry runs never delete, and max_deletes still applies. Other rules' removals wait in the run's plan for review.
